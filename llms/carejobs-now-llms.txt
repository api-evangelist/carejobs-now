# CareJobs.Now

> Hospital, clinic and home care jobs for nurses, doctors, CNAs, therapists, techs, pharmacists and everyone who works in healthcare, pulled every day from employers' own job boards with the pay they post. Search by job, place, shift and pay.

CareJobs.Now is a job board for everyone who works in healthcare: registered nurses and LPNs, nurse practitioners and PAs, doctors, CNAs and patient care techs, caregivers and home health aides, therapists, mental health professionals, pharmacists, imaging and lab techs, surgical techs, EMTs and paramedics, medical assistants, dental teams and the front-office staff who keep care running. It reads the public job boards of 258 US hospitals, health systems, clinic groups and care companies every day, keeps the patient-care, clinical-support and front-office jobs, sorts each into one of 17 kinds of job, shows the pay the posting states and links each job to the employer's own application. It also reads 2 job boards (Adzuna and USAJOBS), labelling each such listing with its board and the employer it names, and lists remote AI-training work for health professionals as one kind of job of its own.

Board as of 2026-10-15: 171,897 open healthcare jobs from 270 employers and programs, 27,624 first seen in the last 7 days (at employers already tracked; an employer's first week on the board, its back-catalog, is not counted as new).
Typical posted pay across today's open jobs: $25-$80/hr, median $40/hr, measured from 42,370 distinct posted hourly rates at 227 employers and programs (110,871 of the 171,897 open jobs post no hourly rate).
Highest-paying healthcare employers right now by the median hourly rate their open jobs post: 1. North American Partners in Anesthesia $190/hr; 2. Montefiore Einstein $100/hr; 3. SonderMind $90/hr; 4. Stanford Health Care $80/hr; 5. OHSU $75/hr. Employers' medians mix every kind of job they have open; the full ranking, and the best-paying employers for each kind of job: https://carejobs.now/highest-paying-healthcare-employers.

## What this site provides
- Open healthcare jobs read every day from 258 employers' own job boards, each linking to the employer's application; a job leaves the board when the employer takes it down.
- The pay each employer posts on its listings, shown as posted (hourly or yearly), before shift differentials and bonuses, and measured into bands by kind of job and by employer.
- A pay-and-jobs page for each of the 17 kinds of healthcare job (/roles/<kind>): what it pays at each employer, who is hiring and its open jobs.
- A plain-language career guide for each kind of healthcare job (/learn/<kind>): what the work is day to day, who it suits, the training and license it takes, and how to start.
- A page for every open job at an employer (/jobs/<employer>/<job-title>) with the full description, every open location, the stated pay and an apply link; the page is removed when the employer closes the posting.
- Pages by US state (/states/<state>), by kind of job in each state (/states/<state>/<kind>), by town (/states/<state>/<town>) and by kind of job in a town: the jobs open there, who is hiring and the pay employers post, with the metro area's BLS wages on town pages.
- Pages by occupation (/occupations/<occupation>, about 150 from CNA to CRNA, specialties such as ICU nurse under their occupation) and by occupation in each state: open jobs, posted pay beside the BLS wage for the nation, the state and its metro areas, licenses and the BLS outlook, each page with its sources and a citation.
- Shift and status labels (nights, days, PRN, part-time, travel) whenever the posting states them, and a search by job, state, shift and pay, with job alerts by email.

## Sourcing and honesty
Jobs come from employers' own public job boards, read every day, plus 2 job boards (Adzuna and USAJOBS) whose listings name the employer and their source; a job leaves the board when it is taken down. Pay is what each posting states, as of 2026-10-15; many postings state none, and salaried jobs are paid by the year, so posted-pay figures describe the listings, not every job in the field. Typical pay is the 25th percentile of what jobs pay (the middle of each advertised range) to the 90th-percentile advertised ceiling. A band measured from the rates of fewer than three employers is labelled as such and never called a going rate. The AI-training programs' stated rates come from their own listings, dated in the directory. Details: https://carejobs.now/methodology.
